# TV Tracker Support

TV Tracker requires iOS 17 or later and an internet connection to search for shows or refresh schedules. Existing subscriptions and watched history remain available on the device when the network is unavailable.

## Common Fixes

- Pull down in Shows to refresh subscribed schedules.
- Confirm the selected time zone under **Settings > Time zone**.
- Search for the full show title when several productions share a name.
- Dates marked **TBA** have not been confirmed by the catalogue provider.

To report a reproducible problem, [open a support issue](https://github.com/2gavy/tvtime/issues/new) with the iPhone model, iOS version, show title, and steps that caused the problem. Do not include passwords, API keys, or other private information.
