# TV Tracker Privacy Policy

Effective August 8, 2026

TV Tracker is designed to work without an account. We do not operate an advertising, analytics, or user-profile backend for the app.

## Information Stored On Your Device

TV Tracker stores your subscribed shows, watched episodes, selected time zone, appearance preference, and cached schedule information on your iPhone. When you are signed into iCloud, subscriptions, watched history, saved show records, and your selected time zone are also stored in your private iCloud key-value store so they can be restored after reinstalling the app and synchronized across your devices. This information is used only to provide the app's tracking features.

## Network Requests

When you browse, search, or refresh a schedule, the app sends the requested title, date, or catalogue identifier to TVmaze and, when configured, TMDB. These providers receive ordinary network information such as your IP address and may process it under their own privacy policies:

- [TVmaze Privacy Policy](https://www.tvmaze.com/privacy)
- [TMDB Privacy Policy](https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy)

TV Tracker does not send your watched history or complete subscription list to the developer.

## Sharing And Tracking

We do not sell personal information. The app contains no advertising SDK and does not track you across apps or websites.

## Data Deletion

Deleting TV Tracker removes its local data from the iPhone. Its private iCloud copy remains available for restoration and can be removed using Apple's system iCloud storage controls.

## Changes And Contact

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Questions can be submitted through the [TV Tracker support page](https://github.com/2gavy/tvtime/issues). Do not include private information in a public support request.
