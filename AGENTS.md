# TV Tracker Product Rules

Use the `viral-product-builder` skill for product strategy, features, pricing, onboarding, UX, App Store presentation, launch planning, and growth work in this repository.

## Product Promise

- Describe TV Tracker as: "Never miss the next episode of your shows."
- Keep upcoming dates for subscribed shows as the single core job.
- Favor a fast chronological list over calendars, dashboards, or generic streaming catalogs.
- Preserve discovery only where it helps users find shows worth subscribing to.

## Product Decisions

- Build a paid product rather than a permanent free tier.
- Prefer one clear lifetime price unless recurring infrastructure makes that unsustainable.
- Keep login optional unless cloud sync or another account-based feature requires it.
- Use concrete numbers such as next-airing time, episode count, and watched hours.
- Add features only when they strengthen tracking, discovery, proof of value, or a privacy-safe sharing loop.
- Avoid copying TV Time or another tracker's branding, trade dress, text, or distinctive interaction design.

## Experience

- Show the schedule immediately; do not add a marketing landing screen inside the app.
- Keep one primary purpose and one obvious action per screen.
- Use plain customer language and avoid explanatory feature copy in the interface.
- Maintain the restrained neutral palette and yellow accent unless evidence supports a change.
- Keep shareable output original and avoid redistributing copyrighted posters, music, or promotional media without permission.

## Release Guardrails

- Licensing, App Store policy, privacy, security, accessibility, and verified data take priority over growth heuristics.
- Test meaningful UI changes on a simulator and relevant iPhone layout before completion.
- Do not commit, push, publish, or install on the user's iPhone without explicit confirmation.
